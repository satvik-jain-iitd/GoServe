Privacy policy

Test version, 25 October 2026. GoServe is in testing. This page is a simple draft and will be replaced by a lawyer-checked page before the pilot.

What GoServe is

GoServe is a food ordering app that we are testing. The people who use it now are our team and our testers.

What we keep

  • Your orders: the dishes, the total, the delivery address you type, the pin code, the time, and your name and phone as saved in your profile.
  • Your saved profile, if you fill it in: your name and phone, a saved address and your pin code.
  • Riders only: the name and phone of the rider, and the rider's place while online, taken from the phone. We keep the latest place and a history of places for 30 days, then delete the history.
  • Only if you allow notifications: an address that your phone's browser gives us so we can send you a notification. It carries no text about you.
  • A random session number made by your phone. It links your orders to your phone. It is not your name.
  • Only if you tap Continue with Google: the name and email that Google gives us. We use them to show who is signed in.
  • On the test version of the app only: which steps you reach (the first open, the code accepted, an address set, an order placed, delivered answered, feedback sent). It carries no name, phone number or address. We do not record your screen.
  • Only if you tap Use my location: the place your phone gives us. We send it once to OpenStreetMap's address lookup to fill in your address. We do not keep the place. We keep the address you confirm.
  • Feedback you send: your words, the screen you were on, a picture if you add one, your phone and browser type, whether you were online, the order number and its status, the app version, and your last taps in the app.

Google sign-in data

If you tap Continue with Google, we get your name, email and profile picture from Google. They are kept in Supabase with your session. They are never shared or sold. They are deleted when you write to us.

Where it is kept

Orders and sign-in details are kept in a database from Supabase. Feedback pictures and reports go to a private Google Drive folder and a private team chat. Only our team can open them.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not send you ads. We use your data only to run the test.

Your choices

Write to us and we will delete your test data. Do not type real card numbers or passwords anywhere in the app. Payment is cash on delivery.
