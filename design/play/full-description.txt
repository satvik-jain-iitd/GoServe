One hot thali. Cooked today. Paid at the door.

GoServe brings the day's plate from a home kitchen near your pin code. Our own rider carries it a short way. You pay cash when it arrives.

How it works
- Type your pin code and see the plate the kitchen cooks today. One veg, one non-veg.
- Add it to your bill and place the order. No card. No wallet.
- Watch it on the slip: cooking, ready, on the way. Call the rider from the slip.
- Pay the rider at the door.

Home kitchens in your pin code
A real kitchen, a short way from you.

Cash first
No card, no wallet. UPI comes later, as a choice, never a must.

Our own riders
No gig fleet. Our rider knows your lane and brings the plate hot.

This is a test version. We start with one kitchen and one pin code and grow one pin code at a time. The sign-in code is fixed during the test.
