GoServe roadmap

updated 01:13 · 25 of 80 stories done

Vision

Hot meals from our own kitchen. At your door in 25 to 35 minutes. Cash at the door. GoServe runs the kitchen, picks the rider, and carries the plate a short way. One pin code at a time. The first kitchen is live in Delhi.
