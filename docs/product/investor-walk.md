# Investor walk (3 minutes, on the owner's iPhone)

Goal: show that one order works from the phone to the door, with the new look. Use the prod app after the release, or dev before it.

1. **0:00 Customer app.** Open `/customer/`. Type the pin code. The kitchen name and "Open till" show. Say: "Only an open kitchen that serves this area is shown."
2. **0:30 Order.** Filter with Veg only, search a dish, add two dishes. The cart bar shows the count and the total. Open it, fill name, phone and address, review the bill. Say: "Cash on delivery, delivery is free, no hidden charge."
3. **1:15 Kitchen app.** On a second phone open `/kitchen/`. The ticket arrives with big type. Tap Accept, Start, Ready.
4. **1:45 Rider app.** `/rider/`. The order is assigned to the nearest free rider. Tap Pick up. Customer phone: the timeline moves and the rider card shows the name and a Call button.
5. **2:20 Notification.** Lock the customer phone. Ready and Delivered show a push.
6. **2:40 Admin app.** `/admin/`. Show the day numbers table: orders, delivered, cancelled, cash.
7. **2:55 Close.** Say: "100 customers, 10 kitchens, 20 riders in one city is the pilot."

Rule: no order id is shown on a customer screen. If one shows, stop and log it.
