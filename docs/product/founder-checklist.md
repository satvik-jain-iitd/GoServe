# Founder checklist — business steps that take time

**Date:** 13 Oct 2026. **For:** the founder. Plain English.
**Status today:** nothing started (owner, 13 Oct). Company, GST and a Razorpay account already exist.

These steps are outside the software. Each one takes days or weeks. The app can be ready before them,
so start them now, in this order.

| # | Step | Why we need it | What to prepare | How long | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FSSAI food licence** | We sell food. Razorpay also asks for FSSAI in the food category | Kitchen address in Delhi, company papers, food safety details | Weeks (UNKNOWN exact) | Before real payments (MVP 2) |
| 2 | **Delhi pilot kitchen** | The first zone, menu and delivery area depend on it | Address, size, staff, equipment | Founder's call | Before the pilot (MVP 1) |
| 3 | **Razorpay KYC** (account exists) | Real UPI and card payments | PAN, company or proprietor proof (any 2 of UDYAM, GST, Shop & Establishment, IEC, postpaid bill), bank account, FSSAI | UNKNOWN (start early) | MVP 2 |
| 4 | **SMS DLT registration** (Jio / Airtel / Vi portal) | Every business SMS in India must be registered: our name, a 6-letter sender ID, and each message template | PAN, business proof, authorisation letter; about ₹5,000–10,000 one time (guides disagree) | About 2–7 working days for the entity, then templates | Phone OTP and SMS (MVP 2) |
| 5 | **WhatsApp Business (Meta) verification** | Order updates and OTP on WhatsApp | Company details, a phone number not used on normal WhatsApp, Facebook Business account | 1–2 weeks (estimate) | MVP 2 |
| 6 | **GoServe domain** (.in at an Indian registrar, .com at Cloudflare) | Brand, login emails, trust; move off letsdwelo.in | Pick the name | Minutes; about ₹500–800 a year for .in | MVP 1 |
| 7 | **Privacy, terms and refund pages** | Razorpay KYC asks for them; users trust them | We draft, founder approves | Days | Before KYC |
| 8 | **Google Play developer account** (only if riders need the Play Store app) | Rider app wrapper | US$25 once; 12 testers for 14 days before going live | 14+ days | MVP 2, if needed |
