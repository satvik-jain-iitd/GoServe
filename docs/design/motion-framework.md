## 2. The criteria and their weights

The weights come from the answers. Each idea gets 0 to 10 on each criterion. Total = weighted sum, shown out of 100.

| # | Criterion | What it asks | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Essence | Does it say "ghar ka khaana, best taste", and not "restaurant" or "delivery"? | 3 |
| C2 | One-second read | Can a stranger understand it in 1 second on a small phone? | 2 |
| C3 | Ownership | Could only GoServe use it? Does it grow out of our own mark? | 2 |
| C4 | System reach | Does it work in all four places: open, landing and ads, order steps, and a still print? | 2 |
| C5 | Ink fit | Indian but minimal: lines, our tokens, no clip art? | 1.5 |
| C6 | Repeat test | Is it still pleasant on the 100th open? | 1.5 |
| C7 | Craft | Can we build it small and fast on a cheap Android phone (SVG or Lottie, under 50 KB)? | 1 |
| C8 | Access and honesty | Does it work with Reduce Motion as one still frame, and promise nothing false? | 1 |

Pass mark: 75. Below 75 is rejected.

## 4. Why the winner wins

- Our pin mark already has an outer shape and an inner circle. Read the outer shape as the tawa and the circle as the roti, and the logo itself becomes home cooking. We do not change the logo; we give it a story.
- Only GoServe can do this, because the end frame is our own mark.
- It works everywhere. App open: the full motion. Landing and reels: the same motion, larger. Order steps: the roti puffs once when the food is Ready. Print and the rider bag: the still mark, which now means "a roti on a tawa".
- A puffing roti is the most home thing in Indian food. It also says "fresh and hot", which is the taste promise.

## 5. Storyboard (about 1 second)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 to 250 ms | A dark iron tawa (a circle, Ink line) with a flat roti on it, on the cream paper. |
| 250 to 550 ms | The roti puffs up once, with one thin line of steam. |
| 550 to 850 ms | The tawa grows its point and becomes the pin's outer shape. The roti settles into the lime inner circle. |
| 850 to 1000 ms | "Go" and "Serve" slide in beside the pin. The menu is ready. |

- A tap at any time skips to the menu.
- Reduce Motion: one still frame, the final mark.
- Night: the same motion under the Night lamp's warm light.
