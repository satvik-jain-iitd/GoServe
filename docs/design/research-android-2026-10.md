# Material 3 and Android Live Updates: the Android side of each Apple rule

Story #328 (D.2, epic E26). It pairs with `docs/design/research-2026-10.md` (Apple, story #327): one row for each of Apple's eight topics, the Android rule that matches it, where it was read, and how sure we are.

**Read:** 27 Oct 2026, from Google's own pages (Tier A: Google wrote them; Google sells Android and Pixel, so a rule can also serve Google, and it does not prove the rule is best for a food app). Pages read: Live Updates, Android 16 features, window size classes, Material 3 in Compose, Android accessibility, Android haptics principles, Compose animation intro, Jetpack Glance. Confidence is for "Google says this and I read it": 0.9 for a page I read in full, less where a page was missing or thin. Four pages could not be read; they are in the Gaps list, and nothing in this file is taken from memory.

## Where Android and Apple differ

- **Touch size:** Apple's regular button is 44 pt; Android asks for 48 dp. One shared token of 48 is safe on both; the design principles (D.5) decide.
- **Smallest text:** Apple's smallest iPhone text is 11 pt; Android says not smaller than 12 sp for body text.
- **The slip outside the app:** iOS Live Activity (Lock Screen and Dynamic Island) and Android Live Update (promoted notification and status bar chip) do the same job, but Android needs a standard notification style, not a custom view. The Living Slip on Android is built from `ProgressStyle`, not drawn freely.
- **End of life:** Apple says to end a Live Activity at once when the task ends (it stays up to four hours on the Lock Screen). The Android page gives no time rule; we end it at Delivered by our own rule.
