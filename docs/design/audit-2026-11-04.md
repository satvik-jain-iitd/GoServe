# Design order, 4 Nov 2026: what the staff apps are missing

The owner asked for this page on 4 Nov 2026, in the bug sprint after release #774.
His words: "do a design order of what buttons are missing on the admin app, the kitchen app and the rider app, and the small design fixes I told you."

How to read it. One row is one screen. "Today" is what the dev link shows now, read from the code on `dev` (file and line). "Fix" says what kind of work it is: a **frame** (a new board on the canvas, the owner says yes first), **code** (the board is right, the app is wrong), or **copy**. "Board" names the canvas board. The canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/6faaULE7F6FDCSQw8YvPuW (row 3b holds the new frames).

## The order of work (critical first, the owner's words of 4 Nov)

1. Simulator: two seed buttons, two groups, Clear everything with the typed confirm. Frames S1 and S2.
2. Sign out and "Signed in as" on the kitchen app, then the rider and admin apps. Frame K6, then two more.
3. Remove a kitchen (K6 bottom), then edit a kitchen on the admin side.
4. Rider removal with old orders (code).
5. The three staff sign-in buttons to match their boards (code).
6. The "New" waiting screen (copy).
7. An empty kitchen is not an offline kitchen (copy + code).
8. The admin error next to the form (copy).
9. One person in two kitchens: the owner's answer first.

Nothing on this list is built before its frame has the owner's yes (ShipQuick `docs/DESIGN_PROCESS.md` rule 2). The sign-out bug #842 stays investigation only until an exact repro.
