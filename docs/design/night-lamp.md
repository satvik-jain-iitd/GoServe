# The Night lamp (idea ID225)

Owner, 27 Oct 2026. Status: idea, logged. Not built. It waits for the owner's pick of a launch motion.

## The feeling

It is late. You are hungry. Somewhere near you, a home cook is still awake.
She turns on the lamp in her kitchen and cooks for you.
The Night theme shows that moment. It says: someone is up, and your food will be hot.

## What you see

1. **The sky.** The page is a deep blue night sky. It is not black and not grey.
   It is the Night paper we already have (`#0b1220`), with small, soft stars on it.
   The stars are few and calm. They do not blink fast. They do not move.
2. **The lamp drops.** A thin wire comes down from the top of the screen.
   On it hangs a ship lamp: a small metal shade with a triangle shape.
3. **The bulb turns on.** The lamp swings a little and stops. The bulb glows.
4. **The light spreads.** A warm triangle of light opens from the lamp down to the bottom of the screen.
   Inside the light the page is a little warmer. The menu sits in the light.
5. **It stays.** The lamp stays at the top while you use the app at night. The light is still. Nothing moves after the first open.


## The owner's second note (27 Oct 2026): warm lights in the night

- Night must not be fully dark. It shows beautiful warm lights: yellow and orange, not pink.
- Think of the night sky and a city seen from a plane window: dark, with warm lights below.
- Think of a Japanese anime night street: dark colours, but one shop is open and warm light spills out of its door. It feels nice that someone is working hard for you.
- The lamp's beam has the shape of a lighthouse beam: a long, soft cone of light.
- So Night gets: the deep blue sky, the soft stars, a few warm yellow-orange glows (open kitchens), and the lighthouse-shaped beam from the lamp. All warm colours come from tokens, at low strength, so text stays easy to read.

## Rules (from our design notes and Apple's and Google's rules)

- **Short.** The whole drop, glow and spread takes under one second. You can tap at any time; the motion never makes you wait.
- **Once.** It plays on the first open of a night session, not on every screen.
- **Reduce Motion.** With "Reduce Motion" on, there is no drop and no spread. The lamp and its light are simply there.
- **Readable.** Text inside and outside the light keeps our Night contrast (AAA). The light only warms the paper a little. It never washes out the words.
- **Our colours only.** The sky is the Night page token. The light uses a warm token (`gs-warm-yellow` at low strength). The stars use the Night hairline colour. No new colour without a token.
- **Calm stars.** At most about 40 small stars on a phone screen, 1 to 2 px, low strength. They are a pattern, not a show.
- **Day is different.** Day keeps the cream paper. The lamp belongs to Night only.

## How it builds on what we have

- Night is already a starry navy sky (owner, 24 Oct). This adds the stars as a real pattern, and the lamp as its one moment.
- It joins two launch ideas: idea 7 (a kitchen light turns on) and idea 5 (the tiffin opens into the menu). At night, the lamp can be the launch motion. By day, the chosen day motion plays.
- On the native apps it can live on in the Live Activity at night: a small lamp glyph next to "Cooking".
